AI Policy as Pedagogy

Co-developing AI course policy with students
Parsa Rajabi, UBC | AI-cademy 2026

This page shares an activity from the AI-cademy 2026 workshop at UBC. It’s for educators who want to involve students in writing their course AI policy, whether or not you attended the session.

About the activity

  • Students work in pairs or trios on short scenario cards about using AI on an assignment.
  • For each card, they pick 🟢 green (allowed), 🟡 yellow (allowed with conditions), or 🔴 red (not allowed), and explain why.
  • The class compares decisions. You then use the conditions students come up with to draft your course AI policy together.
  • It takes about 15 minutes of class time and works on a Miro board or on paper.

What’s in the take-away

  • How to write scenario cards for your own course, with examples from Statistics and History
  • The four cards we used in the workshop
  • A step-by-step plan for running the activity in 15 minutes
  • How to turn the discussion into a course AI policy
  • A personal AI policy prompt for students
