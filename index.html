AI Policy as Pedagogy

Co-developing AI course policy with students
Parsa Rajabi, UBC | AI-cademy 2026

Miro board Workshop slides

Board shortcut: tiny.cc/aicademy-miro

Join the activity

  • Find one or two people near you and share a device
  • Open the Miro board, pick an open group frame, and mark it “In use”
  • Talk through each card, keep the colour your group picked, and delete the other two
    • 🟢 Green: allowed
    • 🟡 Yellow: allowed with conditions
    • 🔴 Red: not allowed
  • If you pick yellow, agree on the condition and say it out loud
  • Finished early? Try the extra cards on the left of the board

Take it back to your course

After the session, use the take-away to run this activity with your own students.
